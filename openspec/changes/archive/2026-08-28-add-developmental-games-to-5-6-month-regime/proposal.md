## Why

The 5–6 month regime currently provides generic wake-window guidance without concrete play ideas suited to developing reach, object exploration, early cause-and-effect understanding, sound play, and floor movement. Adding localized developmental games to each wake block makes the daily regime more actionable while preserving its existing schedule.

## What Changes

- Add the fifteen supplied developmental games as structured content for the 5–6 month regime.
- Assign all fifteen games across the four generated wake blocks using a deterministic `4 / 4 / 4 / 3` distribution.
- Show the assigned games in wake-block details without changing sleep, feeding, ritual, or wake-window timing.
- Translate every game title and full instruction into Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.
- Preserve the supplied safety, supervision, non-forcing, distance, and support meaning across translations.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `regime-developmental-games-5-6-months`: Defines the distribution, presentation, localization, and safety requirements for developmental games in 5–6 month regime wake blocks.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Developmental-game data and wake-block distribution for the 5–6 month regime.
- Localization content for all nine supported app languages.
- Existing wake-block detail presentation, reusing the structured game UI and localization pipeline.
- Focused tests for complete daily coverage, localization, safety wording, regime isolation, and unchanged schedule timing.
