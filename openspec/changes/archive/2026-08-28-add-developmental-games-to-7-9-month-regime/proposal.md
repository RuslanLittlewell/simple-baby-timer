## Why

The 7–9 month regime has two schedule variants but currently offers only generic wake-window guidance. Adding localized developmental games tailored to object permanence, imitation, language, container play, crawling, and supervised water play makes both variants more useful without changing their timing.

## What Changes

- Add the seventeen supplied developmental games as structured content for the 7–9 month regime.
- Support variant-specific game distributions so every supplied game appears once in either schedule variant.
- Use a deterministic `6 / 6 / 5` distribution for the two-nap variant and `5 / 4 / 4 / 4` for the three-nap variant.
- Translate every game title and full instruction into Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.
- Preserve all supplied choking, supervision, stability, water, face-covering, and non-testing safety meaning across translations.
- Leave sleep, feeding, ritual, and wake-window timings unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `regime-developmental-games-7-9-months`: Defines variant-specific distribution, presentation, localization, and safety requirements for developmental games in 7–9 month regime wake blocks.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Regime variant types and localization to support optional variant-level game groups.
- Developmental-game definitions and both 7–9 month schedule variants.
- Localization content for all nine supported app languages.
- Timeline wiring to prefer variant-specific groups while preserving existing age-level groups.
- Tests for both distributions, complete coverage, translations, safety wording, compatibility, and unchanged timings.
