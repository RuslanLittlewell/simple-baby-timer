## Why

The 10–12 month regime currently provides schedule guidance without age-specific developmental play suggestions. Adding localized games for container play, early problem-solving, imitation, language, movement, rhythm, and supervised water play makes each wake block more useful while preserving the existing routine.

## What Changes

- Add the eighteen supplied developmental games as structured content for the 10–12 month regime.
- Distribute the games deterministically across the regime's three wake blocks as `6 / 6 / 6`, with every supplied game appearing exactly once.
- Translate every game title and full instruction into Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.
- Preserve the supplied guidance about realistic expectations, stable equipment, adult supervision, safe movement support, and uninterrupted supervision around water across translations.
- Add the supplied general note that play should use short 5–15 minute episodes and should not become a test of the child's understanding.
- Leave sleep, feeding, ritual, and wake-window timings unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `regime-developmental-games-10-12-months`: Defines distribution, presentation, localization, safety, and general play guidance for developmental games in 10–12 month regime wake blocks.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Developmental-game IDs, definitions, and grouping data.
- The 10–12 month regime configuration and localized timeline content.
- Localization content for all nine supported app languages.
- Presentation of the age-level general play note alongside the games.
- Tests for distribution, complete coverage, translations, safety wording, general guidance, isolation, and unchanged timings.
