## Why

The 6–12 week regime currently labels wake windows as generic activity and does not give caregivers concrete, age-appropriate ideas for interaction. Adding a small selection of localized developmental games to every wake block makes the schedule more useful without making it prescriptive or changing its timing.

## What Changes

- Add the ten supplied developmental games as structured content for the 6–12 week regime.
- Assign two or three games to every generated wake block, rotating the selection across the day so all supplied ideas are represented.
- Show the assigned games in the wake-block detail modal while retaining the existing safety wording, especially for tummy time.
- Localize game titles and instructions for every supported app language.
- Leave sleep, feeding, and wake-window timing unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `regime-developmental-games`: Defines age-specific developmental game suggestions within regime wake blocks and their localization behavior.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Regime data types and the 6–12 week regime definition.
- Regime localization data for Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.
- Wake-block construction and the regime detail modal.
- Focused tests for distribution, localization completeness, and unaffected schedule timing.
