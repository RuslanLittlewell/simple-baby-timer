## Why

The 3–4 month regime currently gives caregivers generic wake-window guidance without concrete, age-appropriate play ideas. Adding localized developmental games to each wake block makes the schedule more useful while preserving its existing timing and optional character.

## What Changes

- Add the twelve supplied developmental games as structured content for the 3–4 month regime.
- Assign two or three games to every generated wake block using a deterministic daily distribution that represents all supplied activities.
- Show the assigned games in wake-block details without changing sleep, feeding, or wake-window timing.
- Translate every game title and instruction into all supported app languages: Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian.
- Preserve the safety and non-forcing meaning of the supplied instructions across translations.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `regime-developmental-games-3-4-months`: Defines the distribution, presentation, localization, and safety requirements for developmental games in 3–4 month regime wake blocks.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Regime content and wake-block game distribution for the 3–4 month age range.
- Developmental-game localization data for all nine supported languages.
- Existing wake-block detail presentation, reusing the structured game UI introduced for younger regimes.
- Focused tests for distribution, localization completeness, safety wording, and unchanged schedule timing.
